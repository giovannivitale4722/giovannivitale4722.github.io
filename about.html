jane street · june 2025

Some Ones, Somewhere

The whole puzzle is nine photos of a board with some coloured tiles on it. The only instruction: the answer is a sentence.

Once I had a hunch about what was going on, I built a tool to test it quickly, and it led me to the answer.

  1. 01

    The hunch

    Letters scattered along the edges of the board spelled out tiling and partridge. A partridge tiling ↗ packs one 1×1 square, two 2×2s, three 3×3s, and so on up to nine 9×9s, perfectly into a 45×45 square. So every photo was probably an unfinished partridge tiling.

  2. 02

    The idea

    Each tiling has exactly one 1×1 square, the "one". If I could finish each board, the one's row and column would give a pair of coordinates, and filling the blank spots along the edges with a repeating A–Z turns those coordinates into letters.

  3. 03

    The tool

    Finishing nine 45×45 tilings by hand is slow and error-prone, so I built an interactive solver to place tiles, check what fits, and test the idea quickly.

    Partridge Tiling solver ↗react · typescript
  4. 04

    The answer

    The nine ones read SU MO FC UB ES IS SQ UA RE. Add the spare tiles sitting on the side table in two of the photos and you get:

    "the sum of cubes is a square"

    which is exactly what a partridge tiling proves: 1³ + 2³ + … + 9³ = 45².
